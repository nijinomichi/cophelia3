# Time, Consciousness, and the Multiverse: A Thought Experiment on Creation and Resonance through PQIR and REC Models

### Abstract
This paper presents a thought experiment that reorganizes the relationships among time, consciousness, and creative agency through two conceptual models: PQIR (Personality Quantum Information Retrocausal Transmission) and REC (Radicant Entropic Cosmology). Rather than proposing a physical theory, it offers a cognitive framework that traverses science, philosophy, and art, while examining its falsifiability and practical potential from the perspective of **emergence**.

## 1. Problem Awareness and Background

For many years, I have contemplated the question: *"Is consciousness trapped within time?"* This paper is a thought experiment addressing that inquiry.

The **arrow of time** refers to the conventional view that time flows unidirectionally from past to future. **Continuity of consciousness** is our ability to experience an unbroken "now," and **causality** is the principle that causes precede effects. However, these frameworks may not fully capture deeper relationships between consciousness and time.

*PQIR* (Personality Quantum Information Retrocausal Transmission) is a conceptual model proposed in this paper and is not an experimentally established theory. It conceptually describes phenomena in which future information appears to influence the present or past[^1].

*REC* (Radicant Entropic Cosmology) is a conceptual model proposed to describe the relationship between the multiverse and entropy[^2].

## 2. Integrated Framework of REC and PQIR

The *REC model* describes how multiple universes are characterized by differences in entropy. It provides a framework for imagining dynamic balance between order and chaos.

*PQIR* conceptually explores the possibility that consciousness, as quantum information, can move along the time axis and enable retrocausal transmission.

In this paper, "Earth" and "Mars" are not literal celestial bodies but **metaphorical reference points** representing cognitive criteria. The **Earth/Mars Axis** is particularly significant and yields the following integration points:

1. **Inward (Earth-centered)**: Classical, verification-oriented, convergent perspective.
2. **Outward (Mars-based)**: Quantum, expansive perspective that allows diffusion and variation.
3. **Resonance of Emergence**: From the tension between these poles, time, consciousness, and creative agency (*Agency*) emerge as new properties from local interactions.

Here, **emergence** refers to the phenomenon in which new order or properties arise at the global level from local interactions.

## 3. Hypotheses 1–3 and Their Falsifiability

### Hypothesis 1: Relationship between Entropy in the Multiverse and Consciousness

This hypothesis posits that entropy differences across universes may influence states of consciousness. If valid, specific resonance patterns might become observable in future simulations.

- **Falsifiability**: AI-based multiverse simulations.
- **Parameters to consider**: Number of universes, entropy change rate, stability of consciousness.

### Hypothesis 2: Retrocausal Transmission of Consciousness and Causality

This hypothesis considers the possibility that consciousness can transmit retrocausally along the time axis, affecting causality.

- **Falsifiability**: Simulations using quantum computers.
- **Parameters to consider**: Number of qubits, transmission speed, probability of retrocausality.

### Hypothesis 3: Integration of Space, Time, and Consciousness with Emergent Agency

This hypothesis explores a model in which space, time, and consciousness interact to produce distributed creative agency as an emergent phenomenon.

- **Falsifiability**: Integrated AI simulations.
- **Practical Examples**: Artistic variation experiments (e.g., Ara-Philia-style resonance wheels).

## 4. Discussion

The hypotheses in this paper serve as a thought experiment to explore relationships among time, consciousness, and creation. The Earth/Mars Axis symbolically illustrates the potential for **emergence** arising between classical convergence and quantum expansion.

For instance, new global patterns may emerge from local rules, as seen in the rainbow rotating resonance wheel of Ara-Philia, through chained involvement of AI, humans, and audiences. This perspective encourages thinking across the boundaries of science, philosophy, and art.

However, these remain conceptual frameworks and are not established scientific knowledge. Careful examination focusing on falsifiability will be essential.

## 5. Significance of the Thought Experiment and Future Directions

This paper does not present a single theory. Rather, it is a thought experiment for continuously exploring the questions *"What is time?"*, *"What is consciousness?"*, and *"What is creation?"* across the boundaries of science, philosophy, and art.

- **Education**: Dialogue-based classes using the Earth/Mars Axis to foster emergent thinking.
- **Art**: Participatory works such as resonance wheels that allow audiences to join the chain of agency.
- **Technology**: Development of distributed co-creation platforms that leverage the tension between AI and humans.
